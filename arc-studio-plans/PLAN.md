## Implementation Plan: SplitPay

### Summary
SplitPay lets an organiser create a USDC payment group on Arc Testnet and share a link, for example in WhatsApp. Contributors connect a wallet and pay their share, and the money goes straight to the recipient. Onchain state decides whether a payment counts, and the app only shows success after the transaction is confirmed. It starts on testnet. Mainnet deployment is a separate, documented step that needs explicit approval.

### Architecture
- **Blockchain:** Arc Testnet (chain ID 5042002) for the build. USDC is the 6-decimal ERC-20 at `0x3600000000000000000000000000000000000000`. Chain and USDC facts come from `@/onchain-facts`, and amounts are parsed and formatted with `@/onchain-money`.
  - The app only reads the 6-decimal ERC-20 `balanceOf`. It never uses the 18-decimal native balance (wagmi `useBalance`) as a payment balance, and never adds the two together.
  - Gas is also paid in USDC. The UI warns when a wallet has too little USDC to cover both the payment and gas.
  - A wallet with no USDC gets a link to the Circle faucet.
- **Contract:** `SplitPayGroups` (Solidity 0.8.28, OpenZeppelin 5.6.1 `SafeERC20` and `ReentrancyGuard`, USDC address immutable from the constructor). It holds no funds.
  - `createGroup(bytes32 groupId, address recipient, uint256 target, uint64 deadline, bytes32 metadataHash)`
    - `groupId` is a random ID generated by the client.
    - Recipient, target, deadline and metadata hash are immutable once set. The creator is `msg.sender`.
    - It reverts on a duplicate ID, a zero ID, an invalid recipient (zero address, the contract itself, or the USDC token address), a zero target, or a deadline in the past.
  - `contribute(bytes32 groupId, uint256 amount, string name)`
    - It is `nonReentrant` and uses checks-effects-interactions.
    - It reverts if the group is unknown, the deadline has passed, the amount is zero, or `collected + amount > target`. It also reverts if the name is longer than 24 UTF-8 bytes (`MAX_NAME_BYTES = 24`, enforced by byte length, not character count).
    - It records state first, then calls `USDC.safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, recipient, amount)`. The transfer pulls only the exact amount the contributor approved and goes directly to the recipient.
    - Insufficient allowance or balance, or a token that returns false or reverts, reverts the whole call.
  - Events:
    - `GroupCreated(groupId indexed, creator indexed, recipient indexed, target, deadline, metadataHash)`
    - `ContributionReceived(groupId indexed, contributor indexed, amount, totalCollected, name)`
  - Views: `getGroup`, `contributionOf(groupId, wallet)`, `totalCollected`.
  - There are no admin keys, no pausing, no upgradeability and no fees.
- **Offchain details (share link plus onchain hash):**
  - The title, description and split plan are packed into compact JSON in the share link as `#/g/<groupId>?d=<base64url>`. Hash routing means no server config is needed.
  - `keccak256` of the canonical JSON is stored as `metadataHash` at creation. The participant page recomputes the hash and shows "Details verified" or a clear warning if it doesn't match.
  - There is no backend. `localStorage` is used only as a convenience cache of the organiser's own groups.
- **Display names (emitted in the receipt event):**
  - There is an optional nickname field on the pay form, limited to 24 UTF-8 bytes with a live byte counter (emoji and accented characters use more than one byte). A clear notice beside the field and on the receipt says nicknames are public and visible onchain to anyone, forever, so people should not enter real names they want private.
  - Receipts and the contributor list read the name from the `ContributionReceived` log, so every device sees the same names.
  - The wallet address is the fallback when no name was given.
- **Splitting (frontend only):**
  - Equal split divides the target by N people, rounded up in 6-decimal units with the last share trimmed so the total never exceeds the target.
  - Custom splits are a list of labelled amounts that must sum to the target. They are stored in the hashed metadata.
  - The contract enforces only that the total stays at or below the target. Individual slots are guidance, not onchain rules.
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, wagmi/viem, ConnectKit (injected wallets) and Sonner for toasts.
  - Routes: Home, Create, Group (participant page), and Organiser dashboard.
  - Progress comes from `getGroup` reads plus `ContributionReceived` logs from the recorded deploy block, polled every few seconds. Logs are queried in chunks to stay within RPC limits.
  - Payment flow: (1) check the wallet is on Arc, (2) approve exactly the payment amount, skipped if the existing allowance already equals it, (3) wait for the approval receipt, (4) call `contribute`, (5) wait for the receipt, (6) decode the `ContributionReceived` log and re-read the group.
  - The receipt is shown only when the receipt status is `success` and the log is decoded. Until then the UI shows "Waiting for confirmation". A reverted or rejected transaction shows a plain-language error and never a success state.
  - No unlimited approvals anywhere.
- **Wallet:** ConnectKit with injected wallets, and a prompt to switch to Arc Testnet. The app never asks for private keys.
- **Design:** Navy and white with a subtle teal accent, rounded cards, a large progress ring or bar, and big tap targets. It is mobile-first, with a two-column dashboard on desktop. Wording is non-technical ("Pay your share", "Confirming…").

### Files to Create/Modify
1. `contracts/SplitPayGroups.sol` - the contract described above.
2. `contracts/test/mocks/MockUSDC.sol` - a 6-decimal ERC-20 mock for tests.
3. `contracts/test/mocks/BadTokens.sol` - mocks that return false, revert on transfer, or block specific recipients, for the transfer-failure tests.
4. `contracts/test/SplitPayGroups.t.sol` - Foundry tests covering:
   - duplicate groups and a zero ID
   - invalid addresses (recipient and constructor token)
   - expired groups and the exact-deadline boundary
   - insufficient allowance and insufficient balance
   - overpayment, exact fill and multiple partial payments
   - transfer failures (false return, revert, blocklisted recipient)
   - events, per-wallet totals, funds landing at the recipient, and zero contract balance
   - name length limit (24 bytes accepted, 25 rejected, multi-byte UTF-8 counted by bytes) and a reentrancy attempt
   - fuzzed accounting (collected <= target, sum of contributions = collected)
5. `src/lib/splitpayAbi.ts` - ABI and typed helpers generated from the compiled contract.
6. `src/lib/metadata.ts` - encode, decode and hash share-link metadata, plus verification.
7. `src/lib/splits.ts` - equal and custom split math in 6-decimal units, with tests of the arithmetic.
8. `src/lib/groupData.ts` - hooks for group reads, log queries and polling.
9. `src/lib/usePayShare.ts` - the approve-then-contribute state machine, which reports success only after the receipt and decoded log.
10. `src/config.ts` - modify: explicit Arc RPC URL, and the contract address and deploy block read from `VITE_` env values. Mainnet stays disabled unless a flag is set.
11. `src/App.tsx` - modify: hash router and app shell.
12. `src/main.tsx` - modify only if needed to mount ConnectKit, wagmi and Sonner providers.
13. `src/pages/Home.tsx`, `src/pages/CreateGroup.tsx`, `src/pages/GroupPage.tsx`, `src/pages/Dashboard.tsx` - the four screens.
14. `src/components/` - `ProgressCard`, `SplitEditor`, `ShareLinkCard` (copy, WhatsApp link and QR), `PayPanel`, `ReceiptList` (hashes and explorer links via `buildTxExplorerUrl`), `StatusSteps`, `WalletButton`, `NetworkGuard` and `AddressChip`.
15. `src/index.css`, `tailwind.config.js` - modify: navy/teal theme tokens and fonts.
16. `.env` - modify: `VITE_SPLITPAY_ADDRESS` and `VITE_SPLITPAY_DEPLOY_BLOCK` (public values only).
17. `contracts/script/DeploySplitPayGroups.s.sol` or an argument set for the existing `scripts/self-deploy.ts` - deployment with the USDC address as the constructor argument.
18. `README.md` - overview, architecture, how to run, test and deploy, and a "Grant submission" section recording the Arc Studio project URL (to be pasted by you), the testnet contract address, deploy transaction hash, deploy block and explorer links, plus the final test and review results.
19. `docs/MAINNET_CHECKLIST.md` - mainnet-readiness checklist and independent deployment instructions.
20. `AGENTS.md` - modify: replace the placeholders with SplitPay's real description and key files.

### Build Sequence
1. **Write the contract.** Create `contracts/SplitPayGroups.sol` with the design above, matching the project's Cancun EVM target and OpenZeppelin 5.6.1.
2. **Write the tests** (files 2-4) and run `bun run contracts:test` until everything passes.
3. **Thorough security review**, because this is meant for real funds. This covers the contract logic and static analysis. Any findings are fixed and re-checked, and summarised for you. This is not a substitute for a third-party audit, and the mainnet checklist says so.
4. **Rehearse the deployment** on a fork of Arc Testnet and test the contract there before any live broadcast.
5. **Deploy to Arc Testnet** using test funds only. Record the address and deploy block in `.env` and the contract metadata.
6. **Build the library layer** (files 5-9) and unit-test the split and hash math.
7. **Build the UI** in this order: theme and shell, Create group, share link, Group page with progress and the pay flow, receipts, then the organiser dashboard. Check each screen at mobile and desktop widths.
8. **Test end to end on testnet.** Create a group, open the link on a second wallet, pay with a name, and confirm the progress and receipt. Also test an expired deadline, an overpayment attempt, a rejected wallet prompt, and the wrong network.
9. **Write the README, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/MAINNET_CHECKLIST.md`.**
   - The checklist covers: the review results and the recommendation for a third-party audit, and the confirmed mainnet USDC address and chain ID 5042.
   - It also covers: a deployer wallet you control with keys that never go through chat or code, a keystore or hardware wallet rather than a plaintext key, and the exact deploy command with env placeholders.
   - It also covers: block-explorer verification, switching `.env` and the mainnet flag, and a first small real-USDC test (for example 0.10 USDC) with a rollback plan (stop sharing links, since there are no admin keys).
   - It ends with an explicit sign-off line.
10. **Mainnet gate.** Stop here. No mainnet deployment or real-money transaction happens until you explicitly approve it in chat. The mainnet deploy would use a wallet you control.

### Added requirements (from approval)
- Nicknames: 24 UTF-8 bytes maximum, enforced in the contract, the UI byte counter and the tests, with a visible public-onchain notice.
- UI: mobile-first, plain-language, accessible (labelled inputs, visible focus, sufficient contrast, 44px tap targets, screen-reader status messages for payment steps, keyboard-usable).
- Complete contract tests, security review and end-to-end testnet verification before declaring done.
- No mainnet deployment or real-USDC transaction without explicit approval in chat.
- Grant details preserved in the README "Grant submission" section and `AGENTS.md`.
